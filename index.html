Guidebook

Training aid for identifying when weather or location may drive alternate planning review.

Controls

  • White dice: random mission.
  • Red dice: random bad-weather practice mission.
  • Magnifier: search airfields by ICAO, name, or city.
  • Compass: guidebook and trigger-point logic.
  • Gear: set the saved departure, destination, and alternates used by refresh.
  • Zap: clear mission fields.
  • Refresh: restore default mission fields.

Logic

  • Destination and alternates evaluate ETA plus or minus 1 hour.
  • Red weather: ceiling below 2,000 ft AGL or visibility below 3 SM.
  • Yellow weather: ceiling at or below 2,500 ft AGL, visibility at or below 5 SM, or wind above 15 kt.
  • Red wind chip: wind above 25 kt.
  • Takeoff review: ceiling below 300 ft flags red; below 200 ft drives takeoff alternate review.
  • OCONUS departure or destination shows an OCONUS chip, but filters focus on weather-driven status.
  • NOTAM feature currently unavailable.

Defaults

Set the mission fields restored by the refresh button on this device.

Sortie Duration

Enter flight time as HHMM. Landing Zulu updates from takeoff plus this duration.

Search Airfield

Type MacDill, MCF, Tampa, or KMCF. If an airfield is not listed, type the ICAO here to use it anyway, or close search and enter it directly.

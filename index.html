Guidebook

Training aid for identifying when weather or location may drive alternate planning review.

Controls

  • Random mission.
  • Random bad-weather practice mission.
  • Code Hunt. Search live weather for METAR/TAF examples like RVR, VV, MPS, TEMPO, BECMG, QFE, and TSRA.
  • Search airfields by ICAO, name, or city.
  • ⚙Set the saved departure, destination, and alternates used by refresh.
  • ✦SIMBA assist. When off, weather answer colors, weather chips, and weather values are hidden so the user must evaluate the METAR and TAF.
  • 📏Open conversion tables for SM/KT equivalents when editing custom limits.
  • ⟳Restore default mission fields.

Logic

  • Destination and alternates evaluate ETA plus or minus 1 hour.
  • Red weather: ceiling below 2,000 ft AGL or visibility below 3 SM.
  • Yellow weather: ceiling at or below 2,500 ft AGL, visibility at or below 5 SM, or wind above 15 kt.
  • Red wind chip: wind above 25 kt.
  • Takeoff review: ceiling below 300 ft flags red; below 200 ft drives takeoff alternate review.
  • Limits can be customized in Defaults; red dice searches against the saved limits.
  • FACTORY LIMITS or CUSTOM LIMITS opens the active limits page.
  • OCONUS departure or destination shows an OCONUS chip, but filters focus on weather-driven status.
  • NOTAM feature currently unavailable.
